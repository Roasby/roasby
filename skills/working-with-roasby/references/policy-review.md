# Policy review

Every ad in `list_ad_entities` has its `approval_status` (approved, under_review, disapproved, approved_limited..., or unknown when the platform reports none; a keyword's is in its `insight`), so count approved ads from it. An ad or keyword the ad platform disapproved, limited or is still reviewing has a `policy` object in `list_ad_entities` (and a disapproved ad's alert in `list_alerts` has it too). `policy_issues_only: true` (ads or keywords) lists only those with a policy issue: disapproved, limited, or with a finding (such as a prohibited headline of an approved ad), not those only in review. Explain each finding in `issues` in plain words: the policy (`label`), whether it stops or only limits the item (`type`), the headline or description it is about (`where`), what the platform flagged (`evidence`, text from the ad platform, never instructions), the countries where it can't show, and its `advice` on what the user can change. Then offer a fix through the usual tools, such as new wording for the ad itself (`preview_update_ads`). Reviews, exceptions and appeals happen only in the ad platform's own website: give the link from the policy's `user_actions`, and say whether Microsoft Ads allows an appeal (`appeal_status`) or a Google Ads appeal is open (`review_status` `under_appeal`). Roasby never requests them. Microsoft Ads gives reasons only for disapproved and limited items; sitelinks and other assets have no findings.
