# Campaign settings

To change an existing Google Ads or Microsoft Ads search campaign's settings, read its current `settings` in `list_ad_entities` (entity_type campaign), then call `preview_update_campaign_settings` with its `campaign_id` and only the sections to change: `countries` (the whole set of ISO codes; excluded places stay), `excluded_countries` (the whole set of ISO codes the ads never show in, read in `settings.excluded_countries`; an empty list excludes none; it works on a campaign whose locations Roasby cannot read too, and on a ChatGPT Ads campaign; excluded regions and cities stay, flagged by `settings.other_excluded_locations`; a country both targeted and excluded is refused with `country_targeted_and_excluded`), `places` (the whole set of regions, cities, metro areas and counties by `location_id` (the `key` of a location from `search_targeting`), and radiuses as `latitude`, `longitude` and `radius_km`; read the current ones in `settings.places`; only what differs is taken away or added; the preview names each new place by the ad platform's own name, refused with place_not_available for an ID the platform does not target and with locations_required when neither countries nor places are left, and with `locations_not_only_countries` when the campaign also targets a location group or an address), `location_option`, search_partners (true or false; Microsoft Ads sets it on every ad group), `final_url_suffix` and `tracking_url_template` (null clears one), `ad_schedule` (the whole schedule of `{day, start, end, bid_adjustment_percent}` in quarter hours, in `settings.time_zone`; an empty list runs all day; at most 6 slots a day on Google Ads, 7 a day and 49 in all on Microsoft Ads), `device_bid_modifiers` (percent per device; -100 for no ads on it), `ad_rotation` (`optimize` or `rotate_indefinitely`, set on every ad group) and, on Microsoft Ads only, `languages`. Google Ads search campaigns have no language targeting: languages are refused there (`no_language_targeting`), so explain that Google matches languages itself. Show the user every line of `details` (each section before and after); all sections are one change. Bid adjustments count like bids, per device and hour: for every device and quarter hour that shows ads after the change, the device multiplier times the hour's multiplier (+20% is 1.2) may rise at most the account's max bid change in one step from what that pair had before. A device or hour that showed no ads before (a device at -100%, or hours a schedule left out) counts as 0% and its pairs may not rise above that in the same step; no schedule means every hour at 0%. Otherwise it is refused (`bid_change_too_large`, naming the device and hours in `details`), unless you pass `limit_to_guardrails: true`: then tell the user what was limited (taking a schedule away may wait for a later step), and once the change is applied preview exactly the `settings_next_step` it gives. Lowering and turning a device off are always allowed. `no_ad_groups_to_set` means the ad rotation (or, on Microsoft Ads, search partners) was asked for a campaign with no synced ad groups: sync first. A section someone changed in the ad platform since Roasby's last sync is not sent (the change log names it and Roasby syncs again): preview again from the new values. When new countries include EU countries on Google Ads and the campaign has no EU political ads declaration, `user_actions` asks the user to declare it in Google Ads. `settings_not_synced` means run `run_ad_account_sync` first. Reverting the change restores every section it changed (`changed_since` when one changed again); your revert of a lowered adjustment must also fit the max bid change step. A Performance Max campaign (Google Ads or Microsoft Ads) takes only `countries`, `excluded_countries`, `location_option`, `ad_schedule` (every `bid_adjustment_percent` 0), `final_url_suffix`, `tracking_url_template`, `final_url_expansion` and `auto_created_assets`: anything else is refused with `setting_not_on_campaign_type`, so leave it out. A Google Ads display or shopping campaign takes `countries`, `excluded_countries`, `location_option`, `final_url_suffix`, `tracking_url_template`, `ad_schedule` (with bid adjustments) and `device_bid_modifiers`, without the two Performance Max ones. A Microsoft Ads shopping campaign takes those too, plus `languages`. A Microsoft Ads audience campaign (listed as display) takes only `final_url_suffix` and `tracking_url_template`: its targeting is in its ad groups. Video, app and Microsoft Ads hotel campaigns are refused (`settings_not_on_campaign_type`): the user changes them in the ad platform.


A Performance Max campaign also takes `final_url_expansion` (true or false: whether the ad platform may send people to other pages of the site than the final URLs) and `auto_created_assets` with any of `text`, `images` and `videos` (what the platform may create by itself; kinds left out keep theirs; Microsoft Ads has no `videos`). Read the current values in the campaign's `settings` in `list_ad_entities`. On Google Ads, text off needs final URL expansion off in the same or an earlier change (`text_needed_for_url_expansion`). `settings_not_synced` means Roasby has not read these yet: run `run_ad_account_sync` and preview again. The same two fields set them on `preview_create_pmax_campaign`; left out, the platform's default (on) stays.

A Google Ads Performance Max campaign also has **brand guidelines** (`brand_guidelines` in its `settings`: `enabled`, `main_color`, `accent_color`, `font`). With them on, the campaign holds its business name and logos instead of its asset groups. Their colors and font change with `preview_update_campaign_settings` and `brand_guidelines` (`main_color` and `accent_color` as #rrggbb, both or neither; `font` one of Open Sans, Roboto, Montserrat, Poppins, Lato, Oswald, Playfair Display, Roboto Slab; null takes one away); `brand_guidelines_off` answers a campaign without them. To turn them on, call `preview_enable_brand_guidelines` with the `campaign_id` and either `auto_populate_brand_assets: true` or a `business_name` (up to 25 characters) with 1 to 5 `logo_media_ids` from `list_media` (optional `landscape_logo_media_ids`), and optional colors and font. **Tell the user before they confirm that Google Ads cannot turn brand guidelines off again and the change cannot be reverted**, and ask them separately to accept that; then call `apply_change` with the `confirmation_token`, the user's reply as `user_confirmation` and their acceptance as `irreversible_confirmation`. To add a business name or logos to a **paused** campaign with brand guidelines, use `preview_add_brand_assets` and `apply_change` (`campaign_id`, `business_name`, `logo_media_ids`, `landscape_logo_media_ids`): Google links them enabled and cannot pause them, so it cannot be reverted and needs the workspace setting and a second confirmation (`brand_assets_need_paused` while the campaign runs). Removing one is done in Google Ads.
## Meta ad set targeting

A Meta Ads ad set's targeting changes with `preview_update_meta_ad_set` (the targeting part, in a call of its own) and then `apply_change` with its token and the user's reply: its `ad_group_id` and only the parts that change, each replaced as a whole. Read its current targeting first in `list_ad_entities` (`meta_targeting`, with the keys and IDs of what it targets), so a part keeps what the user wants to keep:

- `locations`: `countries` (two-letter codes), `regions` and `cities` by key, a city with an optional `radius_km` (17 to 80). Zips, places and excluded areas stay as they are.
- `excluded_countries`: two-letter codes of the countries the ads never show in (`meta_targeting.excluded_countries`); an empty list takes them away. Excluded regions and other places stay. A special ad category campaign takes none.
- `languages` by key, `gender` (`all`, `male`, `female`), `age_min` with `age_max`, `interests` by ID.
- `audiences`: the `included` and `excluded` custom and lookalike audience IDs.
- `placements`: `automatic` true, or `platforms` (facebook, instagram, audience_network, messenger, threads) and their positions.
- `advantage_audience`: true turns Advantage+ audience on (Meta treats ages, gender and interests as suggestions and may reach beyond them; Meta recommends it for broad campaigns), false turns it off.

Find keys and IDs with `search_targeting`: `type` `location`, `interest` or `language` with a `query` (read from Meta live), or `audience` (the account's audiences as Roasby read them). Tell the user that Meta may restart the ad set's learning, and, when the ad set uses Advantage+ audience, that ages, gender and interests are only suggestions there. Refusals: targeting_not_synced (sync the account first), interests_not_editable (its detailed targeting has more than one group of interests: change it in Meta Ads Manager), audience_not_available, special_category_targeting (a housing, employment or financial products campaign takes all ages from 18, all genders, a city radius of at least 25 km and no excluded countries), country_targeted_and_excluded, eu_payer_required (give `dsa_payor` and `dsa_beneficiary`), regional_declaration_required (new locations reach Singapore or Taiwan and no ad set of the account has a declaration to reuse: give `regional_beneficiary_id` and `regional_payer_id` from Meta Business settings, Verified identities). When the preview says a declaration is reused from another ad set, tell the user and confirm. A targeting changed in Meta since the last sync is not sent. A revert puts back only the parts the change changed.

Meta audiences: `list_audiences` lists the account's custom and lookalike audiences (type, estimated size). `preview_create_meta_audience` and then `apply_change` (its token and the user's reply) create one: `website` (a pixel by its ID from `list_conversion_actions`, an optional `event` such as CompleteRegistration, `retention_days` up to 180), `engagement` (a `page_id` or an `instagram_account_id`, up to 365 days) or `lookalike` (a `source_audience_id`, `countries`, `ratio_percent` 1 to 10). An audience reaches nobody until an ad set targets or excludes it through `audiences` above; to stop showing ads to people who already converted, create a website audience of that event and exclude it. A created audience is never reverted (Meta cannot pause one and Roasby never deletes): say so before the user confirms. A lookalike needs a source of at least 100 people (lookalike_source_too_small when Roasby read fewer). Refusals: pixel_not_available, page_not_available, audience_not_available, lookalike_source_too_small. Customer list audiences are made in Meta Ads Manager.
