# Campaign settings

To change an existing Google Ads or Microsoft Ads search campaign's settings, read its current `settings` in `list_ad_entities` (entity_type campaign), then call `preview_update_campaign_settings` with its `campaign_id` and only the sections to change: `countries` (the whole set of ISO codes; excluded places stay; refused with `locations_not_only_countries` when it also targets regions, cities or a radius), `location_option`, search_partners (true or false; Microsoft Ads sets it on every ad group), `final_url_suffix` and `tracking_url_template` (null clears one), `ad_schedule` (the whole schedule of `{day, start, end, bid_adjustment_percent}` in quarter hours, in `settings.time_zone`; an empty list runs all day; at most 6 slots a day on Google Ads, 7 a day and 49 in all on Microsoft Ads), `device_bid_modifiers` (percent per device; -100 for no ads on it), `ad_rotation` (`optimize` or `rotate_indefinitely`, set on every ad group) and, on Microsoft Ads only, `languages`. Google Ads search campaigns have no language targeting: languages are refused there (`no_language_targeting`), so explain that Google matches languages itself. Show the user every line of `details` (each section before and after); all sections are one change. Bid adjustments count like bids, per device and hour: for every device and quarter hour that shows ads after the change, the device multiplier times the hour's multiplier (+20% is 1.2) may rise at most the account's max bid change in one step from what that pair had before. A device or hour that showed no ads before (a device at -100%, or hours a schedule left out) counts as 0% and its pairs may not rise above that in the same step; no schedule means every hour at 0%. Otherwise it is refused (`bid_change_too_large`, naming the device and hours in `details`), unless you pass `limit_to_guardrails: true`: then tell the user what was limited (taking a schedule away may wait for a later step), and once the change is applied preview exactly the `next_step` it gives. Lowering and turning a device off are always allowed. `no_ad_groups_to_set` means the ad rotation (or, on Microsoft Ads, search partners) was asked for a campaign with no synced ad groups: sync first. A section someone changed in the ad platform since Roasby's last sync is not sent (the change log names it and Roasby syncs again): preview again from the new values. When new countries include EU countries on Google Ads and the campaign has no EU political ads declaration, `user_actions` asks the user to declare it in Google Ads. `settings_not_synced` means run `run_ad_account_sync` first. Reverting the change restores every section it changed (`changed_since` when one changed again); your revert of a lowered adjustment must also fit the max bid change step. A Google Ads Performance Max campaign takes only `countries`, `location_option`, `ad_schedule` (every `bid_adjustment_percent` 0), `final_url_suffix` and `tracking_url_template`: anything else is refused with `setting_not_on_campaign_type`, so leave it out. A Google Ads display or shopping campaign takes the same sections plus `device_bid_modifiers` and schedule bid adjustments. Microsoft Ads campaigns other than search, and video and app campaigns, are refused (`settings_not_on_campaign_type`): the user changes them in the ad platform.
