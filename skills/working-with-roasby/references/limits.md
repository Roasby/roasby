# What Roasby can and cannot do

Know the limits before you promise something. Roasby never deletes anything in an ad account.

- **Search campaigns (Google Ads, Microsoft Ads):** a new one holds up to 1,000 ad groups (each with up to 1,000 keywords, 100 negative keywords and 3 ads) and 10,000 negative keywords of its own, within 10,000 operations in all, and targets whole countries. Roasby adds ad groups (with keywords, negatives and up to 3 ads) to an existing search campaign, and keywords and responsive search ads to an existing ad group (all paused); changes a search campaign's settings (countries, regions, cities and radiuses, search partners, tracking, ad schedule, device bid adjustments, ad rotation, Microsoft Ads languages), bidding and conversion goals; edits the text of responsive search ads (headlines, descriptions, pins, display paths, final URL; one by one or by find and replace); and changes a keyword's text and match type (by a replace on Google Ads, where the new keyword starts paused; a match type in place on Microsoft Ads) and its own final URL. It renames campaigns and ad groups.
- **Assets:** sitelinks, callouts, structured snippets and the other text assets, images and business logos from the account's library, and (Google Ads) business names are added to an ad account, campaign or ad group (each linked paused on Google Ads; on Microsoft Ads, which has no paused links, showing at once where the place runs), and their links paused or enabled. An asset's text is never edited, and other ad types than responsive search ads, Meta ads and ChatGPT Ads chat cards are not edited.
- **Performance Max (Google Ads, Microsoft Ads):** Roasby creates campaigns paused and changes asset group texts, images, logos and videos, adds search themes and audience signals, adds asset groups (paused, up to 100 per campaign) and pauses or enables them, and turns final URL expansion and automatically created assets on or off.
- **Keyword research** gives live estimates from Google Ads and Microsoft Ads only (none for ChatGPT Ads or Meta Ads).
- **ChatGPT Ads:** a new campaign targets up to 25 countries and has 1 to 5 ad groups with 1 to 5 ads each. Roasby adds ad groups and ads (paused), edits a chat card's title, body, link and image, renames ads, replaces context hints, changes an ad group's bidding (target cost per click or maximize clicks) and a campaign's countries and platforms. It has no keywords or search terms.
- **Meta Ads:** Roasby creates Traffic, Sales, Leads, Engagement and Awareness campaigns (an ad set with its full targeting and 1 to 5 ads, with media Meta downloads from links, library images or a Page post; carousels and ads with several texts; housing, employment and financial products special ad categories but no political ads), adds ad sets (up to 10 at once) and ads, pauses and enables campaigns, ad sets and ads, edits ads, ad set targeting and end dates, renames, changes campaign and ad set budgets and bid caps, and creates website, engagement and lookalike audiences (a created audience is never reverted). It has no keywords, search terms, negative keywords or assets there; a published ad set's pixel or conversion event, instant lead forms and customer list audiences stay in Meta Ads Manager.

Its other numbers come from a synced copy. When the user asks for something Roasby can't do, say so and tell them what to do instead (usually in the ad platform itself). The docs page `limitations` lists every limit by platform.
