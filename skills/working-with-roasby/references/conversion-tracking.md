# Conversion tracking

Bidding toward conversions and budget advice only make sense when conversions are tracked. Before suggesting a bidding strategy (maximize conversions or conversion value, a target CPA or ROAS) or a budget change, or judging a campaign by its conversions, read `list_conversion_actions` for its ad account (`primary_only`, `include_removed` narrow or widen it) and the campaign's `conversion_goals` in `get_campaign_performance`. Each account says what bidding aims for (`bidding_goals`) and lists its `problems`; each campaign says whether it uses the account's goals or its own (`level`, a `custom_goal_name`) and its own `problems`. Explain each one in plain words: no enabled primary conversion action in the goals bidding uses, a primary action (for example a Google Analytics 4 import) that has not recorded since a date or whose tag is unverified or inactive, a campaign goal without an enabled action, a campaign still bidding on a removed action. A primary action counts toward bidding; a secondary one is only observed. Roasby never changes conversion actions or Google Analytics 4 key events: they are set up in the ad platform (and in Google Analytics), so give the user those steps. What a search campaign bids on can change: `preview_update_conversion_goals` with its `campaign_id` and `level` `account` (the ad account's goals again) or `campaign` with `goal_keys` (Google Ads: the `key` of each goal in the campaign's `conversion_goals.goals` to bid on; every other goal stops bidding) or `conversion_action_ids` (Microsoft Ads: Roasby IDs from `list_conversion_actions`). Roasby never creates a custom goal. On Microsoft Ads a campaign moves neither to nor from the account's goals (`account_goals_not_supported`: Microsoft offers no way to set it back); it changes only between goals of its own. Goals that record nothing under bidding toward conversions are allowed: the preview names each action that is not recording; tell the user. Tell the user each goal the preview says is not recording, and that changing goals restarts automated bidding's learning period. `list_conversion_actions` lists the campaigns with goals of their own in `campaign_goals`. A revert puts back the exact setup before. On Meta Ads, `list_conversion_actions` lists the account's pixels (datasets); each ad set chooses a pixel and an event, so Meta campaigns have no goals to change here.
