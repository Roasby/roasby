# Creating campaigns

## What to ask

`preview_create_search_campaign` and then `apply_change` create a complete search campaign, paused, from one blueprint with one preview and one confirmation. Ask the user for everything it needs (account, name, daily budget, bidding, countries, and the ad groups with their keywords and ads) and always ask whether it contains EU political advertising; never assume the answer. Ask about the optional targeting too, or say what the defaults are: any number of countries; `location_option` (`presence_or_interest` by default, or `presence` for only people in the countries); search partners (off by default); `languages` (Microsoft Ads ad languages as ISO codes, all by default; on Google Ads search campaigns have no language targeting, so explain that Google matches languages itself and the ads should be written in the languages the user wants to reach); and an optional `final_url_suffix` (such as `utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc`) and `tracking_url_template` (with `{lpurl}`).

## The blueprint

The campaign's own `negative_keywords` (up to 10,000) and `ad_groups` (1 to 1,000), each in the shape `preview_add_ad_groups` takes: a `name` unique in the campaign, an optional `cpc_bid_micros` (only under `manual_cpc`, within the account's max bid when one is set; without it the ad group uses the campaign's `cpc_bid_micros`), `keywords` (each with an optional own bid and `final_url`), `negative_keywords` and up to 3 `responsive_search_ads`. Everything is created at once: the campaign paused, its ad groups, keywords and ads enabled under it. It is all or nothing: when any part breaks a rule, nothing is created and the preview names every such part in `details`, with its path (`ad group "Shoes" › keyword: shoes! (Exact match): …`, up to 20 and a count of the rest); fix them all and preview again. The campaign and each ad group, keyword, negative keyword and ad count toward the workspace's items per change (`batch_items`), and the whole campaign (with the budget and each country) must fit one Google Ads request of 10,000 operations (`campaign_too_large`, on both platforms): for a larger campaign, create it with part of its ad groups, then add the rest with `preview_add_ad_groups`. On Microsoft Ads a large campaign is made step by step over a few minutes: it stays pending and can't be enabled until it is complete; if Microsoft stops part way, the change fails with what exists, the campaign stays paused, and Roasby won't enable it (`campaign_incomplete`) until a sync shows every part (its negative keywords, and each ad group with all its keywords, negative keywords and ads; Roasby can't see targeting, so a build that stopped before it stays refused): tell the user what is missing, and that they can finish it in the ad platform and enable it there, or pause it and create it again.

## From a file

Roasby takes no file upload. When the user has one (a Google Ads Editor export, a spreadsheet, a pasted list), read it yourself and fill the blueprint from it, then preview. For example, an Editor CSV with the columns `Campaign, Ad group, Keyword, Criterion Type, Max CPC, Headline 1, Headline 2, Headline 3, Description 1, Description 2, Final URL`:
```
Campaign,Ad group,Keyword,Criterion Type,Max CPC,Headline 1,Headline 2,Headline 3,Description 1,Description 2,Final URL
Shoes,Trail,trail running shoes,Phrase,1.20,,,,,,
Shoes,Trail,waterproof trail shoes,Exact,,,,,,,
Shoes,Trail,free,Negative Phrase,,,,,,,
Shoes,Trail,,,,Trail Running Shoes,Grip On Every Path,Free Returns,Light shoes for rough trails.,Order today and run tomorrow.,https://example.com/trail
Shoes,,jobs,Campaign Negative Phrase,,,,,,,
```
becomes (money in micros, match types in lower case, one ad group per `Ad group`, the rows without a keyword as its ads, campaign-level negatives at the top):
```json
{"name": "Shoes", "negative_keywords": [{"text": "jobs", "match_type": "phrase"}],
 "ad_groups": [{"name": "Trail",
   "keywords": [{"text": "trail running shoes", "match_type": "phrase", "cpc_bid_micros": 1200000}, {"text": "waterproof trail shoes", "match_type": "exact"}],
   "negative_keywords": [{"text": "free", "match_type": "phrase"}],
   "responsive_search_ads": [{"final_url": "https://example.com/trail",
     "headlines": [{"text": "Trail Running Shoes"}, {"text": "Grip On Every Path"}, {"text": "Free Returns"}],
     "descriptions": [{"text": "Light shoes for rough trails."}, {"text": "Order today and run tomorrow."}]}]}]}
```

Add the settings the file does not hold (account, daily budget, bidding, countries, the EU question) by asking the user. Paused or removed rows in the file are still created paused or not at all: ask which ones the user wants. Show every line of the preview's `details` before confirming. The account needs a max daily budget set by people in Roasby. On Microsoft Ads, political ads are refused, manual bidding becomes enhanced cost per click (the preview says so), and negative keywords are exact or phrase only. On ChatGPT Ads, use `preview_create_chat_campaign` instead (no keywords there): ask for the account, name, daily budget, bidding (a max cost per click or maximize clicks), countries (optional), 1 to 5 ad groups, each with its context hints (the topics of the conversations the ads fit) and 1 to 5 chat cards (title, text, final URL and an https image link).

## Meta Ads campaigns

On Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram), use `preview_create_meta_campaign` and `apply_change`: one preview and one confirmation for the whole campaign. Ask the user for:
- the account, the name and a daily budget: on the campaign (the default; Meta spreads it over the ad sets), or with `budget_level: ad_set` the ad set's own (each ad set added later brings its own, none shared; good for fair tests of audiences or creatives; no lifetime budget then);
- the objective: `traffic` (people visiting the links, optimized for link clicks), `sales` or `leads` (website conversions or leads: `pixel_id`, the ID of one of the account's pixels in `list_conversion_actions`, and a `conversion_event` such as `purchase`, `lead` or `contact`), `engagement` (with the ad itself) or `awareness` (reach). Meta's instant lead forms are not created through Roasby;
- `special_ad_category`: ask whether the ads are about housing, employment, financial products and services (credit), or social issues, elections or politics; never assume it. `housing`, `employment` and `financial_products_services` campaigns reach all ages from 18 and all genders, so leave the ages out (special_category_targeting otherwise); social issues, elections or politics ads are made in Meta Ads Manager;
- 1 to 25 countries, optional `age_min` and `age_max` (18 to 65) and dates;
- optionally the rest of the audience, in the same fields as the targeting part of `preview_update_meta_ad_set` (keys and IDs from `search_targeting`): `regions` and `cities` (each with the `country_code` the search gives, so the EU and regional rules see it; a region or city alone needs no `countries`, and one inside a listed country overlaps it), `languages`, `gender`, `interests`, `audiences` (`included`, `excluded`: the account's audiences in `list_audiences`; audience_not_available otherwise), `placements` (`automatic` or `platforms` with positions) and `advantage_audience` (true: Meta treats ages, gender and interests as suggestions). A copy of a campaign is one change this way. Under a special ad category, keep all genders and city radiuses of at least 25 km;
- `page_id`: the Facebook Page the ads run as, one of the account's `pages` in `list_ad_accounts` (read with the conversion setup; a Page the business gives Roasby later shows once the user connects Meta Ads again and the account syncs; the text of `list_ad_accounts` names them with their IDs);
- `instagram_account_id` (optional): the Instagram account the ads run as on Instagram, one of the account's `instagram_accounts` in `list_ad_accounts` or the one linked to the Page (shown with each Page). Without it Meta shows the Page's name and picture on Instagram; the preview names the Page's Instagram account when it has one, so ask the user whether to use it (instagram_account_not_available for an account Roasby did not read);
- when the countries include an EU country, who pays for the ads and who benefits (`dsa_payor`, `dsa_beneficiary`): both are required then (`eu_payer_required`);
- when the countries include Singapore or Taiwan, Meta's regional declaration: Roasby reuses the verified identity IDs of another ad set of the account and the preview names it, so tell the user and confirm; otherwise ask for `regional_beneficiary_id` and `regional_payer_id` (Meta Business settings, Verified identities; `regional_declaration_required`). Given IDs also declare Australia, Thailand, Malaysia and Brazil;
- with an end date, the campaign must run longer than 24 hours (`ad_set_too_short`);
- 1 to 5 ads, each with `primary_text`, `headline`, an optional `description`, an https `link`, a `call_to_action` and exactly one media: `image_url` or `video_url` (with an optional `thumbnail_url`), which Meta downloads from the link (Roasby never downloads or uploads media; a link that does not load stops the campaign before anything is made, and a share link that opens a page, such as Google Drive, Dropbox with dl=0, Google Photos, OneDrive or iCloud, is refused: ask the user for a direct link to the file), `image_hash` (an image already in the account's library: `list_media` lists them, the platform ID is the hash) or `post_id` (an existing Page post as `pageID_postID`, which brings its own text and link, so the ad has no other field).
- An ad with an `image_hash` can take a `vertical_image_hash`: a second library image for Stories and Reels (best 9:16), the first showing in the feeds (best 1:1 or 4:5). An ad with a `video_url` can take a `vertical_video_url` the same way (a 4:5 and a 9:16 video, so Meta crops neither). Neither with more texts or cards. Its texts, link and button can be edited later; its media stay. Roasby does not check the sizes; Meta crops media that does not fit. Such an ad is edited in Meta Ads Manager afterwards.

The campaign is created paused with its ad set and ads active under it, so enabling the campaign starts them; Meta reviews the ads. A video can take a minute or two to process.

When a platform makes only part of a new campaign or ad set, the change fails and what exists stays paused. The failure in `list_ad_changes` starts with what was left and its platform ID ("Left paused: campaign … (Meta Ads ID …)"): tell the user, and finish it instead of creating it again (the same name is refused). On Meta Ads, add the missing ad set with `preview_add_meta_ad_sets` on that campaign (its `entity_id`) or the missing ads with `preview_add_meta_ad`. On Microsoft Ads, finish it in Microsoft Ads or revert to keep it paused. Microsoft Ads accounts without video assets refuse videos for 7 days after Microsoft says so (`video_assets_not_enabled`): leave the videos out. If Meta made only part of it, the change fails with what exists and the campaign stays paused: tell the user what is missing. If Meta's answer was lost, the change fails as possibly made and Roasby never sends it twice: the next sync shows whether the campaign exists, so check before creating it again.

How Meta bids and spends (optional): `bid_strategy` `lowest_cost` (the default), `cost_cap` or `bid_cap` with `bid_amount_micros`, or `min_roas` (Sales only) with `min_roas_percent`. Instead of `daily_budget_micros`, a `lifetime_budget_micros` with an `end_date`: Roasby holds it to the max daily budget per 24 hours of the time left, so a run that starts late today counts as more per day, and enabling the campaign later is checked again the same way (refused with enable_above_cap). A bid cap stays within the max bid, a cost cap within the max target CPA and a minimum ROAS at or above the min target ROAS, once a person set them (`target_above_max_bid`, `target_above_max_target_cpa`, `target_below_min_target_roas`).

To grow an existing Meta campaign of any of these objectives, add one or several ad sets (up to 10, such as one per audience) with `preview_add_meta_ad_sets` and `apply_change` (`campaign_id` and `ad_sets`, each with the fields of one ad set, a list of one for one ad set; one preview and one confirmation, made one after another, revertible alone or as a batch) (its `campaign_id`, a name, the campaign's `objective`, its own countries, ages, Page and 1 to 5 ads, like a new campaign). Under a campaign budget it takes none (`ad_set_budget_on_campaign`); when each ad set has its own budget, give `daily_budget_micros` (`ad_set_budget_required`); when the campaign bids with a cost cap or bid cap, give `bid_amount_micros` (within the max bid and the max target CPA). To add one ad to an ad set, use `preview_add_meta_ad` and `apply_change` (its `ad_group_id` from `list_ad_entities`, a name, the Page and the ad). A new ad set is created paused with its ads active under it, a new ad paused: tell the user that nothing new runs until they enable it, and that reverting pauses it. Meta refuses an ad set it cannot take (another objective, a minimum ROAS or lifetime budget campaign) before anything is made: say why.

A Meta ad can also try several texts: `more_primary_texts`, `more_headlines` and `more_descriptions` (up to 4 each, so 5 with the first); Meta shows each person the mix likely to do best. Such an ad takes an `image_hash` from the ad account's library or a `video_url`, not an `image_url`; its texts must all differ, and it runs only in a Sales, Leads, Engagement or Awareness campaign, not a Traffic one (`text_options_not_on_objective`). Or it can be a carousel: `cards`, 2 to 10, each with a `headline`, a `link` and one of `image_url`, `image_hash` or `video_url` (Meta downloads them); the ad keeps its `primary_text`, `link` and `call_to_action` and has no media or more texts of its own. A post ad takes neither.
