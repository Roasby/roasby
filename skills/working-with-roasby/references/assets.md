# Assets

Read `list_assets` first, then `preview_add_assets` with exactly one of `ad_account_id`, `campaign_id` or `ad_group_id`, or, to add the same assets to several places of one ad account in one confirmation, `campaign_ids` or `ad_group_ids` (up to 20; every asset goes to each). Tell the user when the preview warns that a sitelink goes to another site than the ads where it is linked. Roasby refuses only the assets that break a rule (`detail` names the part) and never links a second asset with the same text to the same place (`asset_exists`: offer to enable the paused one). On Google Ads every new link starts paused: once the batch is applied, offer to enable exactly those links with `preview_enable_entities` (their IDs from `list_ad_changes`). On Microsoft Ads, which cannot pause a link, an asset shows at once where its campaign or ad group runs (the whole ad account: everywhere), so tell the user before they confirm; its revert takes the link away and is not fully reversible (what it served is lost). Callouts show only from 2 to 20 per place, and pausing a link takes it away (enabling links it again; the asset stays). Reverting an add pauses or takes away the links it made, never the assets. Images and business logos are images of the account's library: give `type` `ad_image` or `business_logo` with a `media_id` from `list_media` (pick one whose `fits` suits it: square or landscape images, a square logo). On Microsoft Ads an image needs its alternative text and a logo the business name, both in `text`. A `business_name` (up to 25 characters, `text`) is Google Ads only; on Microsoft Ads the name goes with the logo. A `call` is a phone number in `text` (up to 35 characters) with its `country_code` (US, DE...): Google Ads links it to the account, a campaign or an ad group, Microsoft Ads to a campaign only; `invalid_phone_number` names a broken number or country, and the platform checks the number itself. Google Ads links images to campaigns and ad groups, and business names and logos to the account and campaigns, never to a Performance Max campaign, whose asset groups have its images and logos (`asset_place_not_supported` otherwise); `media_not_in_library` means the ID is not an image of that account's library. A Performance Max campaign takes assets with its `campaign_id` (on Microsoft Ads only while it is paused, like every campaign there); other campaign types are refused (`assets_not_on_campaign_type`).
