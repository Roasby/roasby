# Performance Max asset groups

Google Ads Performance Max campaigns have asset groups instead of ad groups. Read them with `list_asset_groups` (narrow it with `ad_account_id` or `campaign_id`): each has its Roasby ID, status, ad strength, first final URL, headlines, long headlines and descriptions. Images, videos, logos and business names are not listed and are changed in Google Ads itself.

To change the texts, call `preview_update_asset_group_texts` with the `asset_group_id` and the whole new list of any of `headlines` (3 to 15, up to 30 characters each), `long_headlines` (1 to 5, up to 90) and `descriptions` (2 to 5, up to 90). A field you leave out keeps its texts. Characters of Chinese, Japanese and Korean and emoji count as two. Show the user every `details` line: the texts before and after, and that new texts are added as new assets and linked, while texts that go are unlinked from the asset group (their assets stay in the account). `asset_group_texts_invalid` names each problem in the details: fix those texts and preview again. After the user confirms, call `update_asset_group_texts` with the same arguments and the `confirmation_token`. Google reviews new texts before they show.

Only one text change and one signals change per campaign run at a time (`has_pending_change`). A revert links the old texts again and unlinks the ones the change added; it is refused as changed since once the texts changed again. Microsoft Ads Performance Max asset groups are not read or changed.

To guide an asset group toward the right people and searches, add signals with `preview_add_asset_group_signals`: the `asset_group_id`, search_themes (searches it should show for) and `audience_ids` (the platform IDs from the top-level `audiences` of `list_asset_groups`, for that asset group's ad account). A theme the group has already is left out and nothing is taken away; `audience_not_available` means the audience is not one the account has. Tell the user that signals guide Google, they do not limit who sees the ads. After the user confirms, call `add_asset_group_signals` with the same arguments and the `confirmation_token`. A revert takes away exactly the signals the change added.
